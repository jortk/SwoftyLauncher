# 1.9.64

Swofty Launcher for F1 25 1.9.64 lets season cars drive their own handling with equal performance on, and makes the gearbox drive a handling record's gear ratios.

## Addons

- With equal performance on, the Time Trial default, the game takes a car's handling from its base car. A SWOFTY season car named no base car, so it drove the game's F1 2025 car: 8 gears and a 13,100 rpm limiter in a 2013 V8 car. The build now gives a season's base car the values most of its handling records share and names it in each of the season's car layers. A SWOFTY 2013 car in Time Trial drives 7 gears and an 18,000 rpm limiter with equal performance on or off.
- A livery pack for a season, such as SWOFTY 2013 Real, uses the base car of the season it needs.
- A handling record that sets gear ratios or a final drive turns off the car setup's gear ratios for that car, as the game's F2 car does, so the gearbox drives the record's ratios.
- The build log shows a `CarPhysics2/baseVtfAsset` line for each linked car and an `equal performance` line for each base car.
