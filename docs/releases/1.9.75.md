# 1.9.75

Swofty Launcher for F1 25 1.9.75 lets a HUD dial record turn on and off with a leaf, with the stock dial showing while it is off. The timing tower can hold up to 24 rows, and the test shell's `ai on` drives the player's car on F1 25 1.0.145.

## HUD

- A dial record (`hud.element` 1.4) can take its own `switch`: the record shows while the leaf is on, and with `stock: hide` the dial's stock parts show while it is off. Variants without a switch of their own inherit the record's. An addon can therefore show its dial only in its own sessions and leave the stock dial everywhere else. See [HUD elements](/make/hud-element).
- A switch's `default` now sets the start state, before an addon's DLL writes the leaf. `default: false` starts with the record hidden and the stock parts shown. Before, `default` was ignored: the record always started shown and the stock parts hidden.
- `swofty hud check` refuses a `switch` on a screen record, on a scene target, and on a record or variant with `stock: park`.

## Timing tower

- `swofty::timing` holds up to 24 rows (`max_rows`), so a 24-car grid fits in one tower. `TowerLeaves` still declares 22 rows unless an addon asks for more, so existing towers are unchanged.

## Test shell

- `ai on` hands the player's car to the AI on F1 25 1.0.145 in Steam and the EA app. Before, it refused every build except Steam 1.0.141.
