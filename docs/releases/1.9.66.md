# 1.9.66

Swofty Launcher for F1 25 1.9.66 runs onboarding once more with two new steps, the Swofty Launcher EULA and the ads terms with ad privacy choices, moves every ticked addon when one of them is dragged, and gives an unlock row an addon adds a free `statId` that stays the same at every build.

## Launcher

- Onboarding has two new steps, and every player sees it once more. **Swofty Launcher EULA** shows the terms the first **Play** used to show, and **Accept** records them. **Skip** on the first steps leads to the EULA, which can't be skipped.
- The **Ads** step says how ads in the launcher work and links the Terms of Service and the Privacy Policy. Where the law asks for consent, as in the EEA and the UK, it shows the ad privacy form full size, and **Accept** waits until it is answered. The answer covers every ad, so the form no longer opens inside each ad. Players who see no ads skip the step.
- **Settings** › **General** › **Ad privacy choices** opens the ad privacy form again to change the answer.
- Dragging a ticked addon moves every ticked addon with it, also those a search, a filter or a closed folder hides. They keep their order and land together where you drop them. **Escape** during a drag cancels it.

## Addons

- An `add-row` into an unlock list (`unlockable_content.unlockable_items.unlockable_item_list_<n>`) without a `statId` gets a free one from 10000 up, kept in `row-ids.json` so it stays the same at every build. A `statId` that a stock row or an earlier addon row holds moves to a free one, with a warning. Two unlock rows with one `statId` hide each other, and Steam build 26566308 gave 5280 to its own `toro_rosso_barcelona_26` row. See [Unlock items](/reference/ops/database#unlock-items).
