# 1.9.73

Swofty Launcher for F1 25 1.9.73 shows more of which careers a save version holds before you restore it.

## Launcher

- Each version under **Profiles > Saves** names the career played last and how many careers it lists as missing, after its count of careers: `3 careers, newest: Oscar PIASTRI, McLaren, 1 marked missing`. A career counts as missing when that version lists it as **Missing** or **Deleted in game**.
- The **Restore** panel names the careers that version lists as missing on their own line under **You lose**: `Racing Bulls (that version lists them as missing)`. Restoring the version doesn't bring them back, and the notice after the restore says so. See [Saves](/using/career-saves#versions).
