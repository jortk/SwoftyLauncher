# 1.9.71

Swofty Launcher for F1 25 1.9.71 fixes two errors: one stopped **Play** for SWOFTY 2013 and 2014 with some track addons, and the other stopped the launcher from opening at all.

## Launcher

- The launcher opens again when `state/registry.log` in the data folder has grown past 512 KB. It used to stop 3 seconds after starting, every time, when the middle of the log fell inside a character such as `·`. The log is now trimmed at a line.

## Core

- **Play** and **Check conflicts** with SWOFTY 2013 or 2014 and a track addon whose AI data the build can't read, such as Istanbul Park, no longer stop with `bad magic at 0x1d78: expected !sbi or !vbi`. The build copies such AI data for the added team as it is. 1.9.70 introduced this error.
