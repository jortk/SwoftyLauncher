# 1.9.65

Swofty Launcher for F1 25 1.9.65 makes a season list shorter than the stock list it copies hold only the season's cars, fails the build of an addon that adds a third F1 Life car group instead of crashing the main menu, and keeps post-processing on in frames where the HUD skips its read.

## Addons

- A season with fewer cars than the stock list it copies kept the stock list's last cars after its own. SWOFTY 2012's Time Trial list has one car per team, so Haas, RB, Williams and Kick Sauber showed after HRT. The list now holds only the season's cars, and a season with fewer teams than the stock standings it copies holds only its own teams. The boot log shows a `the rest are left out` line for each such list.
- An `add-row` that adds a third car group to `customisation.customisation_cars` now fails the build and says why. The F1 Life scene behind the main menu holds two car groups and picks one by its `stat_id` without a check, so a third crashed the game at the main menu. SWOFTY 2012 1.0.0 and 1.0.1 added one; 1.0.2 does not.

## Graphics

- On track the HUD skips its read of the picture now and then, about 12 times in two minutes of a race. In those frames addon post-processing effects went off for one frame. The passes now run later in that frame instead.
