# 1.9.74

Swofty Launcher for F1 25 1.9.74 downloads the Time Trial plugin and the other signed modules from Swofty's CDN, keeps the cached copy while Swofty's server is busy, and runs the live car operations on F1 25 1.0.145.2472 in Steam and 1.0.145 in the EA app.

## Time Trial

- A signed module, such as the Time Trial plugin (`swofty.tt`), downloads from the CDN link in the server's offer when the offer has one. The link has to be `https`, and the launcher sends no sign-in to it. The launcher checks the file's size, SHA-256 and signature as it does for the server's copy, and downloads from the server when the CDN copy doesn't arrive or doesn't match. See [The Time Trial plugin](/std/systems/laps#the-time-trial-plugin).
- When Swofty's server answers a module download with `too many requests` or a server error, times out or can't be reached, the launcher uses the newest cached copy of that module that still verifies. A download the server refuses, such as one for an account without access, still leaves the module out.
- The launcher's account check every 10 seconds no longer asks Swofty's server for the online session module when your account doesn't have access to it. When that module fails to load in the background, the launcher asks again after 15 minutes, or at once after you sign in again or your account gets access. Before, these requests could leave the server answering the Time Trial plugin's download with `too many requests`: the session logged `module tt-game: not loaded for this session: too many requests; try again later` and recorded no laps.

## Core

- The live car operations run on Steam F1 25 1.0.145.2472 and the EA app's 1.0.145 as well as on Steam 1.0.141: `cars::live` reads and writes, the `car.built` and `car.reset` events, layers and `car.profile` records, the car levers (`engine.power_scale`, `engine.limiter_offset_rpm` and `ai.grip_scale`), the AI racecraft fields (`ai.<group>.<key>`) and the fuel mixes that addons such as Rich Fuel Mix change. Before, they ran on Steam 1.0.141 only. See [Changing a car live](/std/systems/car-physics#changing-a-car-live).
- `loader.log` names the build they run on: `[cars] live car operations on (Steam 26566308): <n> fields`, with `EA 1.0.145` or `Steam KB` for the other two. On any other build, the EA app's 1.0.141 among them, they stay off: `[cars] live car operations off: build <timestamp> (image 0x<size>) has no checked car layouts`.
