# 1.9.77

Swofty Launcher for F1 25 1.9.77 fixes the handling data of season add-ons whose cars change their radiators, and the season box that **Continue** loads in Grand Prix.

## Seasons

- The cars of SWOFTY 2011, SWOFTY 2012 and SWOFTY 2013, and their Real Liveries add-ons, load their own handling again. Before, the game logged `/rad_upgrades/0/changes/0/op "=" is string, expected integer` for each car and ran them without it, so their engine temperatures and slipstream did not apply. The core now accepts the string operations a car's radiator changes use.

## Grand Prix

- **Continue** in Grand Prix loads the season box of the saved weekend. Before, a weekend saved from a season box could continue with another season's content active.
