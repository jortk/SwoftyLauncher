# audio: the game's Wwise integration

Owner: see `subsystems.tsv`.

## Map
- Wwise job workers. The sound engine init 0x143fbf830 gives Wwise `Snd_Request_JobWorker` (0x143fbdd00) as its request-job-worker callback. The callback hands each requested worker to an idle mailbox of the 12 audio job worker threads (mailboxes of 0x30 bytes at 0x146c63bd0: +0x00 thread id, +0x08 fn, +0x10 wake event, +0x24 job type, +0x28 busy). It queues the rest in `g_Snd_JobWorkerOverflow` (24 slots of 0x30 at 0x146c63e10, first free) and counts them in `g_Snd_JobWorkerPending`, all under `g_Snd_JobWorkerLock`.
- `Snd_Run_JobWorkerThread` (0x143fc6500) runs its mailbox request, then loops while the pending count is non-zero over overflow slots 0-11 only. A request queued in slots 12-23 never runs: every worker spins in that loop, the Wwise audio thread (0x144ce2a40) waits forever for its job group while holding the Wwise main lock 0x145fbd440, and the next `AK::SoundEngine::SetRTPCValue` that finds the command queue full blocks a game job. The watchdog (0x1443e0b80) kills the process after 30 s with the crash stub 0x14391f740 (writes 0xdf3b9606 to address 1). Seen in a 1990 race on leaving a replay (session 20261030-113325).
- The core's audio job queue fix (native/src/core/audio_queue.cpp, default on, SWOFTY_AUDIO_QUEUE_FIX=0 turns it off) wraps `Snd_Request_JobWorker`. It passes a thunk in place of each worker function; after every request it handles, the thunk moves queued requests from slots 12-23 into free slots 0-11 under the lock. The request wrap does the same after each call.

## Track audio
Static on d873a1c8 plus offline decoding of 14 stock track ERPs (2026-11-17 engine-re round D).
- Per-track audio is placement data in the track ERP only. `audio/2025/audio_memory_map.xml` loads metadata (boot) plus sfx and music (persistent), and there are no per-track banks. The event names used by the track layers (play_sfx_pit_horn, play_amb_garage) are FNV-1 ids in metadata.bnk; aux buses (reverb_garage) and audio materials (wall_concrete, surface_grass) are in Init.bnk.
- Layers (World.layers of `<t>_common.wep`): audio_env (AudioEmitter, AudioMultiEmitter + AudioSpeaker, TriggerZone + Trigger, e.g. the pit horn), audio_crowd (template AudioCrowd = component AudioF1Crowd), and audio_occlusion_next_gen (AudioRoom + AudioPortal reverb zones per garage; AudioGeometry occluders for hills, grandstands, overheads, pit walls and garage walls). In all 14 stock tracks the layers trackmarkup_audio, true_passby and trackmarkup_audio_corner_assist / _whoosh_left / _whoosh_right are present but empty. audio_crowd and audio_occlusion_next_gen are missing on some stock tracks (las_vegas, losail, miami), so they are optional.
- Readers: AudioEmitter `Game_Read_AudioConfig_2` 0x1443e6db0 (misnamed; audio.event.01..10 become Wwise ids through `Game_Get_WwiseIdFromString`, plus audio.param.NN, playImmediately, playOutsideTrackOnly, addDoppler). AudioMultiEmitter key registration `Game_Read_AudioConfig_4` 0x1443f0aa0 (misnamed; registers the keys events 01..10, emitters 01..40 = AudioSpeaker woids, podium emitters 01..05; verify V11305: it is an init routine, not the reader). `Snd_AudioRoom_ReadProperties` 0x1443f5cc0 (AUX bus name, room name, send level, wall occlusion, priority, events and stop events 01..05). `Snd_AudioPortal_ReadProperties` 0x1443f3240. `Snd_AudioGeometry_ReadProperties` 0x144520200. `Snd_AudioF1Crowd_ReadProperties` 0x1409f3330 (excitement zone links the emitter to a crowd excitement zone).
- Open: the emitter runtime (which Wwise game object, RTPC and occlusion calls) and what an unknown event name does at runtime. Statically it gives an id with no event in the bank; Wwise normally reports an error and plays nothing (T231).
