## Question
Does corner-cutting / track-limit detection use the TrackSpace strip index (point2..point3 middle strip vs side strips) or the wheel surface?

## Hypothesis
Statically each XML TrackSpace volume becomes 3 strips (`Game_TrackSpace_AddVolumeFromGates` 0x1445f2b60): index 1 = point3..point2, 0 and 2 = the side strips out to point4 / point1. `Game_TrackSpaceTracker_UpdateEntry` 0x142f5e6d0 copies the strip index into entry+0x10 per car (1 when outside). The corner-cutting grading (`Sess_Rules_CornerCuttingTimeGained` 0x14359e5a0, sig session.rules_corner_cutting_time_gained) reads per-car records, and the settings say num_wheels_required_to_be_on_track, which hints at a per-wheel surface test. Expected: cuts are flagged while the strip index stays 1 (surface-based), and the strip index is used for something else (pit/limits HUD or AI).

## Promotes
- `Game_TrackSpaceTracker_UpdateEntry` (strip index semantics) to L3.
- Notes on `Sess_Rules_CornerCuttingTimeGained` / `Sess_CornerCutting_Update` (detector input) to L3.

## Expected log lines
- `strip 1 -> [02]` near the cut / run-wide distance -> the strip index follows the car over the white line.
- `corner_cutting_time_gained` call at the cut -> the grader ran.
Pass rule: both seen and their order shows whether the cut call needs a strip change. Fail rule: no strip changes at all while off track -> the TrackSpace gates are wider than the white lines on this track (inconclusive; repeat on another track).
