# T158 Does the in-game sun direction follow the sky/sunHeightAngle and sky/sunRotationAngle curves, or the solar position from the track latitude?

- Requested by: engine-re, 2026-11-17
- Status: draft
- Session: (filled by the lead when batched)

## Question
At a fixed track and clock time, does the sun direction in the light constants equal the values of the bound `sky/sunHeightAngle` / `sky/sunRotationAngle` curves, or the elevation/azimuth from `Game_TimeOfDay_ComputeSolarAngles`?

## Hypothesis
Static reading (render.md#lighting): two consumers bind the curves (`Render_SkyState_BindTodCurves` owner +0x780 / +0x750, `Render_SkyRenderer_BindTodCurves` +0x800 / +0x7d0), and `Render_SkyState_UpdateSolarSun` computes the solar position from the track weather config latitude. Expected: the curves give the direction, and the solar result only rescales the day (sunrise/sunset).

## Promotes
- `Render_SkyState_BindTodCurves`, `Render_SkyRenderer_BindTodCurves`, `Render_SkyState_UpdateSolarSun`, `Game_TimeOfDay_ComputeSolarAngles` (L3 on pass; on fail the stretch hypothesis in render.md is dropped)

1. Start a Time Trial-free offline Grand Prix practice at two tracks with different latitude (e.g. Bahrain and Silverstone), same clock time (custom time of day), dry.

## Expected log lines
- `sun_block lat=` with two different latitudes -> proves the latitude reaches the sun model.
- `sky_curve height=` per frame -> proves which values the renderer holds.
Pass rule: the logged curve height/rotation equals the direction derived from the light constants (lead compares with a frame capture). Fail rule: they differ while the solar result matches.
