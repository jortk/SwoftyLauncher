# T222 Which box-entry and box-exit spline does a car get, and does the neighbour box decide it?

- Requested by: engine-re, 2026-11-17
- Status: draft
- Session: (filled by the lead when batched)

## Question
For a pit stop, which builder fills the car's entry spline (car record +0x118) and exit spline (+0x128), and does the choice follow `Game_PitLane_IsBoxSafeToCross` on the neighbour box?

## Hypothesis
Static (d873a1c8):
- Entry: `Game_PitLane_HandleEnterPitBox` picks one of three builders.
  - 0x1434c0600 is pull-through-preceding-box. It is used when the neighbour box (box ±1 by garage_order_reversed) is safe.
  - 0x1434c0fb0 is pull-in-normal. It is used when the neighbour box is busy.
  - 0x1434c1780 is spline-to-marks. It is used under flag bit 2 of 0x1413ca0d0.
- Exit: `Game_PitLane_BuildBoxExitSpline` 0x1434bf2d0 uses 0x1434c2870 (pull-through, record +0xd4 = 3) when the neighbour box is safe, and 0x1434c1c00 when it is not.

## Promotes
- Game_PitLane_BuildBoxExitSpline, Game_PitLane_IsBoxSafeToCross, the state-builder notes on 0x1434c0600/0x1434c0fb0/0x1434c1780/0x1434c1c00/0x1434c2870.

Pass rule: the builder follows `safe`. Fail rule: a builder is used that the safe flag does not predict.
