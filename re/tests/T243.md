## Question
For one track: the value of `weather/atmospherics/ibl_keyframe` (binding gws+0x900), the four key indices and the two blend weights that `Game_GameWeatherSystem_UpdateFrame` (0x1438b9ff0) builds (key range gws+0x1f24..+0x1f28, overcast offset gws+0x1f2c, turn flag gws+0x1f00), and the `sky/IBLReflectionIntensity` value written to renderer+0x178, at morning, noon and evening sessions, dry and wet.

## Hypothesis
Static reading (render.md#time-of-day-curve-files): the track ships 20 IBL keys (key0..key19 cube arrays, 14 probes each); ibl_keyframe maps the time of day to a fractional key (stock timeofday_debug curve 0 at 09:00 to 5 at 24:00), the key pair is mirrored after the turn time and a second pair offset by +0x1f2c is the overcast set; the weather weight comes from the active weather dataset curve, split at 0.5.
