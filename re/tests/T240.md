# T240 What exposure does the eye adaptation produce per time of day, and which tonemap threshold is active?

## Question
In a race session, what are the values of `g_sceneLuminanceAndExposure` [0] adapted luminance, [1] exposure, [4] frame luminance and the `toneMapSettings` fields toneMapThreshold / MinLum / MaxLum / MaxSceneLum / AdaptationSpeedDL / LD, at a few clock times?

## Hypothesis
Static reading (render.md#tonemap-and-exposure): `E = (5/6) / (8 * clamp(A, MinLum, MaxLum) / 2^Threshold)`, where A is the adapted 15-stop histogram mid-percentile luminance. The threshold comes from the ToD keys postprocess/tonemap/threshold_a / threshold_b / threshold_lerp (garage and tunnel variants for those areas).
