## Question
In a wet race, which values does the 96-byte `g_trackConstants` buffer (cb2) carry (fp32_absorbtion, fp32_wetness, fp32_puddles, fp32_dryingLine, dynamicTrackInfoEnabled), and how far do they darken the road albedo and lower its roughness in the G-buffer?

## Hypothesis
The track_surface DXIL (`shader_package_2025/track_surface.nefx2.sm51.erp`, entry `gbuffer_export_ps`) reads cb2 at these offsets: wetness +20, puddles +24, dryingLine +28, absorbtion +16, dynamicTrackInfoEnabled +76.
- With dynamic track info enabled, wetness becomes `saturate(max(0.45 * saturate(w * 2.222), noise * 2 - 1 + w))`.
- Albedo is darkened by `1 - absorbtion * wetMask`.
- Puddle roughness goes toward about 0.63 or 0.13 (see render.md#wetness).
- Expected: dry race = all four values 0; wet race = wetness > 0, and puddles > 0 only in heavy rain.
