# T213 How much GAME_DEFAULT does a track's AI tables take, and where is the cap?

- Requested by: engine-re, 2026-11-17

## Question
Is the AI-table crash (fatal heap error 0xc 'GAME_DEFAULT' with 334 MB of VPTimes in one merged ERP, 239 MB loads) the GAME_DEFAULT virtual heap limit of 390 MiB (195M x (100 + 100) / 100), and how much of it do VPTimes use?

## Hypothesis
Static (round B2): Sess_VirtualPerformance_IngestVpTimes 0x1438a5100 copies every VPTimes block into the VIRTUAL_PERFORMANCE heap, a redirect of GAME_DEFAULT (memory_map.xml: 195M win release/master, VIRTUAL_PERFORMANCE size 85M); GAME_DEFAULT inherits virtualExpandPercent 100 from the memorymap root (inheritance is a hypothesis), so its limit is 390 MiB shared by all GAME_DEFAULT redirects.

## Promotes
- `Sess_VirtualPerformance_IngestVpTimes` to L3; the session.md section "AI tables: VPTimes, ERSBoost, brake settings and their heaps" from hypothesis to confirmed.

## Build

- data: (a) a stock track, (b) the 239 MB merged package (Hockenheim old R22/R46 class), (c) optionally the 334 MB one.

## Trace config
1. After load, call the GAME_DEFAULT heap vtable +0x60/+0x68/+0x70/+0x80 (capacity, used, free, largest free; the fields Ne_ReportMemoryError 0x14395f6e0 prints) once in the grid, heap from the registry 0x14612a410 vt+8('GAME_DEFAULT'); also for 'VIRTUAL_PERFORMANCE'.
2. Hook 0x1438a5100 entry: log size (r9) per call and the sum.

## User steps
1. Load (a), Grand Prix race to the grid. 2. Same with (b). 3. Optional (c) until the crash.

## Expected log lines
- capacity 390 MiB (409,600 KiB) for GAME_DEFAULT; used grows by about the VPTimes sum between (a) and (b).
Pass rule: capacity = 390 MiB and (c) fails when used + request > capacity. Fail rule: other capacity (then the inheritance hypothesis is wrong) or VIRTUAL_PERFORMANCE has its own 85M cap.
